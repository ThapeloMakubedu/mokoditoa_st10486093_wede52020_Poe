Our story

Divine Delights was started in June 2026 by Wanga Ndou, a home based baker in Pretoria West. What began as baking for family and friends grew into a small business built on care, honesty and good cake.

What we stand for

Mission

To bring joy to every occasion with cakes that are baked with care and priced honestly.

Vision

To become the name people in Pretoria West think of first for custom cakes.

Who we bake for

Anyone in and around Pretoria West planning a birthday or celebration who wants a cake made just for them.

How an order works

  • 1. Send your design, flavour and date through the order form
  • 2. We reply on WhatsApp with a quote
  • 3. You confirm and pay a deposit
  • 4. Your cake is baked fresh and ready for collection
